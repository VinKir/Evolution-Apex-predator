# Enemy AI and spawning

- An enemy type is just a prefab with `OrganismCombatant`, `OrganismProgression`, `EnemyAIController`, `OrganismMovementMotor` and `OrganismBody`, plus hitbox children. There is no template SO. To create one:
  1. Make a Prefab Variant of an existing enemy (`Assets/Prefabs/SimpleEnemy`).
  2. Set `behaviourType` and AI tuning on `EnemyAIController`, and corpse settings on `OrganismCombatant`. Keep the `OrganismBody` empty and the characteristics at 0; spawned enemies get them from generation.
  3. Add it to an `EnemySpawnZone` entry (`prefab` is typed `EnemyAIController`) with weighted evolutions, or hand-place it with a `BossController`.
- Spawned enemies are generated by `EnemyGenerator.Generate(evolution, zone.dangerModificator)` (`Controllers/AI/EnemyGenerator.cs`), which returns an `EnemyBuild`:
  - The path goes from the picked evolution down to stage 0, choosing a random entry of `previousEvolutions` at each step.
  - Characteristics are the sum of every stage's bonuses on the path. Organs are the union of every stage's `organs`, plus 0..`dangerModificator` organs per stage from its `purchasableOrgans`.
  - Each part's level is random in 0..`LevelCap`: a 50% chance of `[LevelCap-5, LevelCap]`, otherwise below it.
  - Free mutations are the `mutations` of every stage on the path, plus one random variant per milestone (every 5 levels) the part reaches.
- `OrganismCombatant.ConfigureEnemy(level, build, groupId)` applies the build through `OrganismProgression.InitializeRuntime` (which clamps the level to `LevelCap`) and `OrganismBody.ReplaceParts`.
- Bosses and the player are configured by hand in the inspector: `currentEvolution`, `level` and characteristics on `OrganismProgression`, organs on `OrganismBody`, and `factionGroupId` on `OrganismCombatant`.
- Enemies spawn only from `EnemySpawnZone`s (Circle or Box, with an absolute level range and weighted prefab and evolution entries). `EnemySpawner` ticks every `tickInterval`:
  - All distances come from the camera, in one place: `MinSpawnDistance` is the view's half-diagonal plus `offscreenMargin`, so it follows the player's size through the camera ortho size. `MaxSpawnDistance` = min + `despawnBuffer`/2, and `DespawnDistance` = min + `despawnBuffer`.
  - It spawns only in the overlap of the zone and the ring [min, max] around the player (`EnemySpawnZone.TryGetRandomPoint`), clear of `obstacleMask`, and caps each zone at `maxAlive`.
  - It calls `OrganismCombatant.ConfigureEnemy(level, EnemyGenerator.Generate(...), groupId)` and then `EnemyAIController.Initialize(player, hasHome, homePosition, homeRadius)`.
  - It culls only enemies it spawned, once they're beyond `DespawnDistance`. Leaving the zone doesn't despawn an enemy.
- Zone flags work through the `OrganismProgression` achievement flags:
  - A zone stops spawning when the player has all of its `disableWhenFlags`.
  - An optional kill counter (any death or player kills only) grants `killRewardFlag` at `requiredKills`.
  - Flags and counters are session-only.
- `BossController` (`Controllers/AI`) goes on hand-placed enemies:
  - It stays dormant until the player is within `activationDistance` or damages it.
  - Past `leashDistance` it returns to its anchor via `EnemyAIController.ForceReturnHome`, then calls `Deactivate()` and `OrganismCombatant.RestoreFull()`.
  - On death it sets `rewardFlag`. The spawner never culls bosses.
- Every `thinkInterval`, `EnemyAIController` runs `AIContext.UpdateFrom` (perception), then `OrganismBrain.DecideState` (a decision tree per `OrganismBehaviourType` using `AINeeds`), then `AIStateMachine.ChangeState`.
- All states are in `AIStates.cs`; to add one, also add an `AIStateType` value and a case in `AIStateMachine.CreateState`. `AIMemory` tracks the last attacker.
- Attack and eat ranges scale with `Stats.sizeMultiplier`.
